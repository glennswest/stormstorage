## 3. Techniques for open issues

- #44 (live migration of a VM's disks, waits on stormblock#295): QEMU/Proxmox practice is
  a mirror job in write-blocking copy mode, so the mirror is guaranteed ready when the
  source is inactivated; without it a mirror can be not-ready at cutover
  ([Proxmox patch](https://lists.proxmox.com/pipermail/pve-devel/2022-September/054140.html)).
  Worth stating in the #295 contract: `synced` must mean "every new write also reaches the
  target before it is acked" (or the cutover step must re-check `bytes_remaining == 0`
  after quiescing), not just "the bulk copy finished". Our planned `cutover` already stops
  the mirror after `synced`; it should refuse unless the state is still `synced` at that
  moment.
- Dirty-bitmap handling across a migration (QEMU iotests 194, libvirt Jan 2026
  [series](https://patchew.org/Libvirt/cover.1769622307.git.pkrempa@redhat.com)) is only
  relevant if stormblock later keeps change tracking for #46 backups across a handover.
